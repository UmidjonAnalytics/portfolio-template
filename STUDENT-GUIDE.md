# Student guide — your portfolio in 15 minutes

This is your own website. You do not need to touch any code or database. Everything is done in
the **admin panel**, and the public site updates the moment you press **Save**.

Your site has two addresses:

- **Public site** – what visitors see: `https://<your-site>.vercel.app`
- **Admin panel** – where you edit: `https://<your-site>.vercel.app/admin`

---

## 1. Log in

1. Open `/admin` (add `/admin` to the end of your site address).
2. Type the email and password your teacher gave you.
3. Press **Sign in**.

Forgot the password? Press **Forgot password?**, type your email and check your inbox (also the spam folder).

When you are inside you will see the **Dashboard**. It shows a checklist of what is still missing.
Follow it from top to bottom.

---

## 2. Fill in your profile (do this first)

Go to **Profile** in the left menu.

- **Name** – your full name.
- **Job titles** – one per line, for example `Data Analyst` and `Excel enthusiast`. They rotate in the big heading.
- **Tagline** – one sentence about what you do.
- **Bio** – 2–3 short paragraphs. Use the toolbar for bold or lists.
- **Availability** – choose "Open to work" if you are looking for a job.
- **Contact details** – add at least your email or Telegram. Empty fields are simply hidden.
- **Photo** – drag a photo into the box. Square or portrait photos look best.
- **CV (PDF)** – drag your CV PDF into the box. A "Download CV" button appears on the site automatically.

Press **Save** (top right or bottom right).

### The three language tabs (EN / RU / UZ)

Above every form you see **EN · RU · UZ**. Write the English version first. Then click **RU** and
**UZ** and write the translations. If you leave a translation empty, visitors of that language
simply see the English text — nothing breaks.

---

## 3. Add your first project

Go to **Projects** and press **Add your first project**.

Fill in:

1. **Title** – short, for example `Sales dashboard for a small shop`.
2. **One-line summary** – what you did and the result.
3. **Domain** – Retail, Finance, HR, Education…
4. **Tools** – click the tools you used (Excel, Power BI, SQL…).
5. **Problem / Approach / Result** – three short paragraphs. Write them like a story: what was wrong, what you did, what changed.
6. **Key metrics** – 2–3 numbers, e.g. value `-30%`, label `Reporting time`.
7. **Cover image** – a screenshot of your dashboard or chart.
8. Turn on **Featured project** for your best one.

Make sure **Visible on the public site** is switched on, then press **Save**.

Open your public site in another tab: the **Projects** section and the **Projects** link in the menu
are now there. Add more projects the same way. You can drag projects up and down to change the order.

### Optional: interactive chart

In the project form scroll to **Interactive chart**, switch it on and press **Insert example**.
Replace the numbers with your own. Visitors will be able to switch between bar, line and area and
hide/show each series.

---

## 4. Add a certificate later

You can add certificates any time — even months later.

1. Go to **Certificates** → **Add your first certificate**.
2. Type the title (for example `Google Data Analytics`), the issuer (`Coursera`) and the date.
3. Paste the verification link if you have one.
4. Drag a screenshot or photo of the certificate into the image box.
5. Press **Save**.

The **Certificates** section and menu link appear on your site right away. Visitors can click the
picture to see it in full size and press **Verify**.

The same works for **Experience**, **Education**, **Testimonials** and **Blog**: the section is hidden
while it is empty and appears with the first saved item.

---

## 5. Change your photo or CV

Go to **Profile** → **Photo & CV**.

- To change the photo, hover over it and press **Replace**, or press the trash icon and drop a new one.
- To change the CV, drop the new PDF into the box. The old one is replaced.
- Press **Save**.

---

## 6. Messages

When someone fills in the contact form on your site, the message appears in **Messages**. The number
of unread messages is shown in the menu. Open a message and press **Reply by email**.

---

## 7. Colors, language and dark mode

Go to **Settings**.

- **Accent color** – pick one color for buttons and highlights.
- **Default language** and **Default theme** – what new visitors see first.
- **Sections** – switch a whole section off if you do not want it, even if it has content.
- **SEO title / description** – the text Google shows for your site.

## 8. Design

Go to **Design**. The preview on the right shows your site with the changes before you save.

- **Style** – the look of the whole site: Classic, Classic B/W, Excel, SQL, Power BI or Notebook.
  Picking one also picks a matching accent color, which you can change.
- **Background, Transitions, Spacing** – pattern behind the page, how blocks appear while
  scrolling, and how much space sits between them.
- **Blocks** – pick how each block looks (for projects: grid, small tiles, large one under another,
  carousel or swipe) and change their order with the arrows.
- The phone and moon buttons above the preview show the site on a phone and in dark mode.

---

## Tips

- **Draft vs Published** – the eye icon next to every item. Drafts are only visible in the admin panel.
- **Order** – drag the handle (⋮⋮) on the left of any item.
- **Nothing shows up?** Check the item is published and refresh the admin page. Changes normally appear within a second.
- **Demo data** – Settings → Danger zone → *Seed demo data* fills the site with an example analyst so you can see how everything looks. *Clear all data* removes everything so you can start clean.
- Keep images under 2 MB. They are compressed automatically.
